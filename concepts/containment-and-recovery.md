# Containment and Recovery (Beginner Notes)

After a security incident is confirmed,
SOC teams take steps to limit damage
and restore systems.

## What is containment?
Containment is the process of stopping
the attack from spreading further.

### Examples of containment
- Blocking a malicious IP address
- Disabling a compromised user account
- Isolating an affected system

## What is recovery?
Recovery is the process of restoring
systems back to normal operation.

### Examples of recovery
- Resetting passwords
- Restoring systems from backups
- Applying security patches

## Why both are important
- Containment limits immediate damage
- Recovery ensures long-term stability

## My understanding
Containment is about control,
recovery is about restoration.
